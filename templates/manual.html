Operating Instructions

  • 01
    Capture Image — Point the rifle-mounted NoIR camera at the target area and tap Capture Image. The system will acquire and save a timestamped JPEG automatically.
  • 02
    Capture Video — Tap Start Recording to begin. Tap Stop Recording when done. The footage is saved as an MP4 with a timestamp filename.
  • 03
    View Images / Videos — Browse all saved captures from the Saved Images and Saved Videos tiles on the main menu. Individual files can be deleted from within the gallery.
  • 04
    Change PIN — Change your 4-digit access PIN at any time. You will need your current PIN to confirm the change.
  • 05
    Settings — View system status, camera health, and available storage on the device.
Do not power off the device while recording is in progress. Always tap Stop Recording before shutting down.
